ClipTok Studio

ClipTok Studio is a personal desktop tool used by the streamer Songon_BE. It runs locally on the creator's own computer, turns recordings of their Twitch live streams into vertical short videos (captions, framing, music) and lets the creator send those videos to their own TikTok account, either as a draft in the TikTok inbox or as a post they configure and confirm themselves.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the creator's own TikTok account is connected to it.
