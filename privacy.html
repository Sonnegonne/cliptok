Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What the app accesses

When you connect your TikTok account, ClipTok Studio receives, through TikTok Login Kit, an access token and refresh token, your TikTok open ID, and basic profile information (display name, username and avatar) shown so you know which account will receive a post. With the video.upload and video.publish permissions, the app uploads the videos you choose to your account.

Where data is stored

Everything stays on the computer where the app runs: tokens are saved in a local file, videos are created and stored locally. There is no server, no database and no analytics. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data sent to TikTok

Only the videos you decide to publish, together with the caption and settings you choose, are sent to TikTok through the official TikTok API.

Twitch data

To find highlights, the app may read the public chat replay of the owner's own Twitch videos. This data is processed locally and is not shared.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect TikTok from the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the app's folder removes all local data.
